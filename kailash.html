The Kailash Mansarovar Yatra · by the Lipulekh

Kailash Mansarovar

Set out from Delhi, where the yatra assembles, by road into the Kumaon hills and up the gorge of the Kali to the last camp under Om Parvat. Cross the Lipulekh Pass into Tibet on foot, take the yatra's bus round Mansarovar for the snan, and walk the three days round Kailash, over the Dolma La.

  1. Om Parvatॐ in the snow above Nabhidhang, the last camp in India
  2. MansarovarThe snan in the sacred lake, 4,590 m
  3. Yam DwarThe gate of the parikrama, under Tarboche's flags
  4. DirapukThe north face of Kailash
  5. Dolma LaThe pass, about 5,630 m
  6. DarchenThe parikrama complete
