Spiritual Traveller

Set out from Pune with a staff and a lit diya and travel across India by motorbike, train and jeep, and the last miles on foot: up into the Sahyadri, south to the seven hills, then north through the night to the high Himalaya. Pause for darshan at each shrine.

  1. BhimashankarJyotirlinga in the monsoon forests of the Western Ghats
  2. Tirumala TirupatiSri Venkateswara on the seven hills
  3. KedarnathThe highest Jyotirlinga, 3,583 m, under the snows
  4. BadrinathBadri Vishal on the Alaknanda
